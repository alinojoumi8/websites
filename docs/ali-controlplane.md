# Site brief: ali-controlplane

- **Goal:** a sixth portfolio for Ali Nojoumi, aimed at Director of Product and VP of Product roles in payments, digital asset platforms and applied AI systems. Success is an email or a LinkedIn visit.
- **Audience:** hiring managers, recruiters and founders in fintech. They decide inside two minutes. Also design-literate visitors who judge craft.
- **Pages / sections:** one page, plus a print page that makes the PDF dossier.
  1. Hero: the studio portrait runs the full height of the screen and fades into the page. A glass lens follows the pointer and shows the composite photo. The "Inspect leadership stack" switch opens the lens fully and swaps the headline for a process table of the small Alis. Each small Ali is a hotspot with one proof.
  2. Four figures on a glass strip docked to the bottom of the hero, with spring counters.
  3. Three case studies. Each has a wide Krea cover, then a pinned workbench with a Product strategy view and a System architecture view: Pontemesto (isometric payment routing you can run), Keywa (two-layer threshold signing you can operate, with signer replacement), CMOR Group (headline sentiment engine) plus the three other ventures.
  4. The lab: one console with three small demos that run in the browser and link to GitHub (Manyworld, TrialLens, CrowdLens).
  5. Skills matrix with pill filters, education, languages.
  6. Contact on a full gold field with magnetic actions and the PDF dossier.
- **Style / mood:** pinned by Ali as "Horological Fintech Precision", built this time as a control plane, not as a watch. An architect's scale model of a financial system at night: obsidian `#07090E` and carbon slate `#0F141C`, warm gold `#D4AF37`, brushed steel `#94A3B8`, live emerald `#10B981` and cyan `#38BDF8`. Syne for headlines, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for data. The ground is an etched grid floor under a warm gold key light from the upper right, the same light as the portraits. Diagrams are isometric models in the same language as the Krea renders. A rail on the left edge carries a packet down the page as the visitor scrolls. It must not look like `ali-caliber`, which came from the same brief, nor like the ledger, banknote, model-shop or atlas sites.
- **Content:** resume and cover letter dated October 2026, plus two photos, all supplied by Ali. Krea makes the art: monogram, payment routing, threshold vault, simulation network, sentiment engine. Raw files live in `sites/ali-controlplane/raw` (git-ignored); `scripts/images.mjs` rebuilds the shipped files and holds the prompts. Facts live in `sites/ali-controlplane/PRODUCT.md` and `src/lib/content.ts`.
- **Never on the site:** the same hidden items as the first portfolio, and no resume PDF. The dossier PDF is made from the site's own page, so it holds only public facts. The repo is public, so these notes do not spell the hidden items out. Ask Ali for the exact list.
- **Features:** React island in Astro, Tailwind CSS, Motion (Framer Motion), GSAP ScrollTrigger and SplitText, Lenis. Motion presets live in `src/lib/motion.ts`. The two main diagrams are layered SVGs with named ids and are also served as plain files under `/exports/` for motion tools. Full layout for phones, reduced motion and no JavaScript.
- **Hosting / domain:** not published yet. Build with `SITE_URL=https://<domain> npm run build` so the canonical link and the share image are absolute.

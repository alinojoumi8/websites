# Site brief: ali-nojoumi

- **Goal:** get Ali into a conversation about a product leadership role in payments and digital assets. Success is an email or a LinkedIn visit.
- **Audience:** hiring managers, recruiters and founders at fintech, payments and digital-asset companies. They decide inside two minutes.
- **Pages / sections:** one page, built as an open account book. Fo. 1 The Account. Fo. 2 The Staff. Fo. 3 Pontemesto. Fo. 4 Keywa (with a two-layer signing demo). Fo. 5 Earlier Books. Fo. 6 Experiments (with a small double-entry economy). Fo. 7 Open Entry (contact). Marbled back endpaper.
- **Style / mood:** double-entry ledger. A different form stock per folio. Red and black ink, cobalt stamps, marbled endpapers. Besley display and text, Courier Prime for figures, one clerk's hand. Photo cut-outs of Ali as paper puppets.
- **Content:** resume and cover letter dated October 2026, plus two photos, all supplied by Ali. Krea makes the extra poses of the tiny Alis. Facts live in `sites/ali-nojoumi/PRODUCT.md`.
- **Never on the site:** the phone number, the company valuation figure, the resume's wording for one client sector, and the resume PDF. The repo is public, so these notes do not spell out the hidden items. Ask Ali for the exact list.
- **Features:** scroll-turned pages, tiny Ali staff layer, live marbling (WebGL), signing demo, economy demo, "post an entry" form that opens an email. Full stacked layout for phones, reduced motion and no JavaScript.
- **Hosting / domain:** undecided.

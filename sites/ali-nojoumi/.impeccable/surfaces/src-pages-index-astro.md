# Surface: Ali Nojoumi portfolio

Scope: whole site, one page. Visitor mode: Experience.

Audience: hiring managers, recruiters and founders in payments and digital assets, between meetings, two minutes to decide.
Job: see who he is, believe the range, feel the proof, start a conversation.
Action: "Post an entry", a working form that opens an email to Ali. LinkedIn and GitHub beside it.
Proof: resume facts in PRODUCT.md, exact scope and dates. Illustrative demos are labelled as illustrations.
Constraints: no phone, no valuation figure, no client-sector wording from the resume, no resume PDF. Complete stacked layout for phones, reduced motion and no JavaScript.
Memorable moment: the first page turn folds the printed giant Ali in half while the tiny Alis hop to the next spread.
Unresolved: domain, hosting.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ali's career is one set of double-entry books. Every venture posts twice, what was missing in red and what shipped in black, and a staff of tiny Alis keeps them. It refuses the dark hero, stat row, timeline and project-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: One open account book fills the viewport, hinged on a centre gutter. One form stock per folio: eye-ease green, goldenrod, flamingo pink, cobalt, manila, carbon black. Cobalt feint rule on every row, vermilion column rules, black and red ink, cobalt rubber stamps, marbled fore-edges and endpapers. Besley Black display, Besley text sitting on the rules, Courier Prime posting figures, one clerk's hand. Photographic cut-outs of Ali move as paper puppets. No cards, pills, icon tiles or badges.

STORY: The first spread states who he is and balances what was missing against what shipped. The payroll proves the range: seven jobs, all named Ali. Pontemesto and Keywa post the proof. The visitor signs a two-layer withdrawal and runs a small double-entry economy. The last row is left open for them; posting it opens an email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed ledger spread on 25 rows. Folio links are thumb-index tabs on the right edge, the only map of the book. "Ali Nojoumi" in Besley Black across both pages, rows 1 to 5. Giant cut-out Ali straddles the gutter from row 6 and bleeds off the bottom, printed on both pages. Left page rows 6 to 13 in red: what was missing. Right page rows 6 to 13 in black: what shipped. Tiny Alis stand on his shoulders and on the rules. Primary action "Post an entry" on the left page, rows 15 to 16, clear of the tiny Alis below it. A page corner invites the turn.

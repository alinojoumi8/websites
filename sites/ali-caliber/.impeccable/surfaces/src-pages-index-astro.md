## Direction contract

THESIS: A watch shows one calm face, and the work happens in the movement behind it. The page is that watch: the executive in front, the mechanism open for inspection. It refuses the dark dashboard portfolio of equal glass cards around an avatar.

OWN-WORLD: Restrained-to-committed dark. Obsidian ground cut with wide horizontal dial grooves that catch a moving sheen. One photographed steel cushion bezel (a Krea render, cut out, nine-sliced) frames the portrait. Panels are smoked-glass plates with one polished top edge, because the brief pinned glass. Minute-track ticks are the rules; sub-dial registers carry the figures; date windows carry dates. Gold is the hand and the one active part. Emerald means live or approved. Cyan means data in transit. Syne heavy for names, Inter for prose, JetBrains Mono for data only.

STORY: Visitor meets the face, inspects who does what, then operates three mechanisms (route a payment, sign with thresholds, score a headline), runs three simulations, checks fit, and writes to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT (as built, with the reason for each change from the first draft): The portrait sits right of centre inside the steel bezel, about half the viewport wide and three quarters high, with a minute track and a live gold seconds mark around it. It is cased, not faded into the ground, because the hotspots must stay locked to the photo and a watch needs a case. "Ali Nojoumi" in Syne 800 sits upper left on the dial grooves, two lines, anchored to the top so it does not move when the stack opens under it. The cover-letter sentence is under the name, then gold "Email Ali" and steel "See the work". The inspect switch rides on the top-left corner of the portrait (under it on phones), so it stays under the pointer when it is flipped. A minute track along the bottom edge carries the live Toronto time and the cue to scroll. A steel loupe follows the pointer over the portrait.

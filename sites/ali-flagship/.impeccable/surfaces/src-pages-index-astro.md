# Surface brief: ali-flagship home

Scope: the single page of `sites/ali-flagship`. Mode: Experience.

Audience and job: fintech hiring managers and founders, two minutes, deciding if Ali is worth a conversation. Action: email or LinkedIn. Proof: the resume figures, each with scope and date. Constraints: hidden items in PRODUCT.md, no fabricated proof, WCAG AA, full reduced-motion and no-JS page.

Process pinned by the user: Krea art first, shown to him, then code. No decision page was served; the assigned direction was built unattended. Build path: code-led (recorded default). The roll was run from the workspace root (`/mnt/data/projects/websites`), so `concept-seed --check-seed` knows the seed from there, not from the site folder.

Signature interaction: the pointer is a lamp. It slides the foil (copper to emerald) everywhere. Over the hero window, where an engraved plate of the portrait rests, it shows the colour photograph with the tiny Alis. Motion grammar: print passes. Headings land out of register and snap true. Frames keep two hairline passes out of register at rest; they snap true under the pointer.

Raises kept from declined challengers: depth by stacked passes only, never soft shading (layered papercut). Colour confined to foil, floods and one stripe; text stays achromatic (iridescent cloud edge). Scale jumps from microtext to billboard in one view (hybrid collage).

Recorded adaptations after the first finish review:
- The engraved plate is the resting state in the hero only. "Hold it to the light" keeps the colour photograph at rest, because the audience must see the real face once with no hover, on touch screens and with scripts off. Its lamp shows the watermark.
- The practice section keeps four panels with a pointer glow, because the user's brief pins "mouse-tracking radial glow on cards". They are built as proofs: square, inner rule, out-of-register passes, no soft shadow, staggered.
- The serial is `AN 2018 2026`: his initials, the year of his first venture and the current series. It is set twice in the first viewport at two sizes.

Unresolved: domain, hosting. Until a site URL is set at build time, `og:image` is relative and the canonical link is left out.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ali is a mint. The page is the plate room where one founder's record is engraved and issued. It refuses the dark grid of glowing cards and the headshot-beside-headline hero.

OWN-WORLD: Plate-black navy ground. Live engraved guilloche hairlines in WebGL. Bone ink. One optically variable foil, copper to emerald, driven by the pointer. Emerald and copper floods own whole sections. Wide black grotesk filled with engraving lines, microtext rules, serial numbers in mono.

STORY: The visitor sees a founder who issues real things, believes the figures because each carries scope and date, and writes to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The name runs billboard-wide across the full width in engraved-line fill whose bands roll under the pointer. A tall polymer portrait window sits right of centre with a foil stripe across it; the lamp shows the tiny Alis inside. Role line and serial sit bottom-left. The "Start a conversation" action sits bottom-right. A guilloche rosette turns behind everything.

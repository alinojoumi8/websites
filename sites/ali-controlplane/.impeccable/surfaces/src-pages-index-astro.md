# Surface: the portfolio page (`src/pages/index.astro`)

Scope: the whole one-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a hiring manager for a Director or VP of Product role, between meetings. They must believe inside two minutes that Ali has done every job between a customer need and a live financial product, then email him or open his LinkedIn. Proof on hand: four resume figures, three companies, three open-source projects, two photos. Constraints: the look is pinned by the brief; three resume items stay off the page; demos are labelled toy models.

Memorable moment: the lens. A glass lens rests on his shoulder and shows two small Alis at work before the visitor does anything. The "Inspect leadership stack" switch opens it over the whole portrait, and the headline becomes a process table wired to all eight.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ali is the control plane. One person runs every process between a customer need and a live product, and the page lets the visitor inspect those processes. It refuses the framed-headshot hero with a name beside it and a row of equal project cards below.

OWN-WORLD: an architect's scale model of a financial system at night. Obsidian floor etched with a fine grid, one warm gold key light from the upper right (the light in the portraits and in every Krea render). Isometric slabs with cast shadows and right-angle light rails draw every diagram, on their own opaque floor. Gold is the person and his actions; inside a toy it also marks the item that needs a human look (a new signer, a sceptical reader, an excluded source). Emerald is money in motion and approval. Cyan is simulation data. Steel is structure. Syne sets headlines, Inter text, JetBrains Mono machine readouts only: identifiers, numbers, states and ledger rows. The page ends on a flat gold field that carries the rendered titanium monogram.

STORY: who he is and his claim, in one sentence; the stack of jobs he does himself; four numbers with scope; three systems the visitor can operate; three lab demos; skills tied to proof; one clear way to reach him.

FIRST VIEWPORT (as built, with the reason for each change from the first draft): the portrait stands the full height of the stage, flush to the right edge, with no frame; its left edge feathers into the floor and a soft out-of-focus copy of the photo carries its light past the top and under the strip. One headline sentence starts with his name in gold and overlaps only the dark edge of the photo; the column narrows on tall windows so the copy never crosses his face or hand. Under it: "Email Ali" in gold and the "Inspect leadership stack" switch. A glass strip with the four figures is docked across the bottom, over the blurred continuation of the photo, because the sharp photo must stop above it: one small Ali sleeps on its bottom edge. A rail on the left edge maps the page and ends in a gold email button, which replaced the floating email pill because the pill covered text. Toronto time and "open to roles" sit top left on desktop (top right is his face) and under the actions on phones.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: a glass lens rests on his shoulder from load and shows the composite photo through it; it follows the pointer and returns when the pointer leaves. The switch grows that same lens over the whole portrait; on wide screens the portrait steps back if the table needs the room; the headline swaps for a process table; each small Ali gets a hotspot, an elbow leader that never crosses a face, and a tag. The selected tag opens into a glass callout with the capability; the proof reads in the table.

MOTION GRAMMAR: light travels on rails. A packet runs down the left rail with the scroll. Rails in each diagram draw in as the case study is pinned and scrubbed. Counters settle on springs. Nothing bounces; everything else eases out.

FORM: brief-pinned direction (the brief's own "live control plane"), built as an isometric system model. Roll skipped: the direction was pinned by the user's brief, so no concept roll ran and no seed reference exists.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

What is CaseIQ?

CaseIQ is a Canadian litigation support service for people representing themselves in court. It combines full-file review, court document preparation under a licensee on its team, forensic accounting, handwriting and signature analysis, and procedural coaching. CaseIQ helps you prepare and present your own case. It is not a law firm.

Where CaseIQ helps: Toronto, Ontario and across Canada

From our Toronto base, we support self-represented litigants in courts across Canada, including the Ontario Superior Court of Justice and Small Claims Court, the Supreme Court of British Columbia and the Court of King's Bench of Alberta. Rules and licensing differ by province, and Quebec differs most, so ask us first.

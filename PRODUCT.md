Scope: this record describes CaseIQ, the first site in this workspace (`sites/caseiq/`). Other sites will need their own record.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Single-file responsive page: HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons, vanilla JS (requested in the brief). Hosting undecided.

## Users

Self-represented litigants in Canada. They are either priced out of a traditional retainer or were burned by a lawyer who did not read the file, billed heavily, and left them without direction. They arrive anxious and sleep-deprived, holding a messy file, often against a deadline.

## Product Purpose

CaseIQ is a litigation support, legal education, and forensic analysis service. It turns overwhelming facts and evidence into a clear, chronological, judge-ready case the litigant can present. Success means a visitor books a free 15-minute triage or submits the intake form.

## Positioning

The founders have been self-represented themselves. CaseIQ reads every page of the file and pairs preparation with forensic work (accounting, handwriting and signature analysis) that ordinary self-help resources do not offer.

## Operating Context

Motions, factums, affidavits, pleadings, notices of application, trial records. Canadian court procedure, which varies by province.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: case storytelling and strategy; court document drafting and preparation; full document review and file audits; forensic accounting; handwriting and signature verification; litigant education and courtroom coaching.
- A lawyer or licensed paralegal is on the team and drafting happens within that licence's scope (user-confirmed). The licensee's name, licence type, and jurisdiction are not yet supplied. Before launch, the wording must match what that licence actually permits in each province served.
- CaseIQ is not presented as a law firm providing formal legal representation. Disclaimer is required.
- Toronto is the home base, serving Canada-wide (owner chose the "Canada-wide, Toronto as home base" option).
- Undecided: pricing, domain, hosting, intake form destination, booking tool, street address, contact details.

## Brand Commitments

Name: CaseIQ. Voice: speaks from lived experience, direct, angry on the reader's behalf but never defamatory. Binding visual direction from the brief: deep navy/obsidian, warm parchment/ivory, high-contrast gold/emerald accents; editorial serif headlines with crisp sans-serif body.

## Product Principles

1. Read everything. The promise that separates CaseIQ is attention to the actual file.
2. Never promise an outcome. Courts decide; CaseIQ prepares.
3. The litigant stays in command and always knows the next step.
4. Proof over adjectives: claim only what can be shown.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Users are stressed and may be on phones or older devices. Plain language, high contrast, full keyboard support, and reduced-motion support are required.
